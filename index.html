What Arca is

A trusted vault for the life you're building.

Local-first

Your data stays on your machine. No cloud sync by default, no telemetry, no analytics phoning home. SQLite under the hood, JSON snapshots in app storage, daily backups. If you ever stop using Arca, your data is yours to take with you.

Focus + planning, in one

The productivity spine: capture tasks, run focus sessions with a Pomodoro timer, log real time on real work, review your week with deep-ratio and closure metrics. Built around execution, not just lists.

Health · Wealth · Wise

Arca is evolving from a productivity tool into a four-domain personal operating system. Health (body composition, recovery, sleep). Wealth (net worth, cash flow, holdings). Wise (reading, meditation, languages). All in the same warm, calm surface.

AI on your terms

An optional AI coach that runs against the providers you choose — Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or a fully local Ollama setup. Your prompts and your data don't get bundled into anyone's training set.

Designed to be lived in

Warm parchment surfaces, deep navy ink, a single bronze accent. The mark is a vault-arch — two converging strokes meeting at a keystone, with a four-pillar base. Read the visual brief if you want the long version.

What it is not

Not a SaaS. Not a team-collaboration tool. Not a multi-user platform. Arca is a personal operating system, deliberately scoped to one user, deliberately scoped to deep work and the life that surrounds it.
